1. What we collect

  • Account info: the phone number or email, nickname, and membership status you provide when signing up / in — used to deliver account and membership services.
  • Usage stats: usage counts such as collection / download totals — used for product improvement and quota metering.
  • Sign-in state: a login cookie shared with the Account Center, used to recognize your sign-in status inside the extension.

2. What we do NOT collect

  • We do not collect or upload the pages you browse or the page images themselves; images are downloaded to your local device only when you actively click.
  • We do not record your browsing history or track the sites you visit.

3. How data is used & shared

  • Used solely to provide the services above; we do not sell or share your personal information with third parties.
  • Payments are processed by licensed payment providers; we do not store your payment card details.

4. Storage & security

  • Account data is stored on our servers with reasonable safeguards; local preferences and collection history are kept in your browser storage on your device and can be cleared by you at any time.

5. Your rights

  • You may access, correct, or delete your account; upon deletion, the related personal data is removed.
